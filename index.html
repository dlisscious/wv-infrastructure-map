MARL — MidAtlantic Resiliency Link

  • Developed by NextEra Energy Transmission, selected by PJM
  • 500-kV, ~107.5 miles, Dunkard Twp PA → Gore VA
  • WV counties: Monongalia, Preston, Mineral, Hampshire
  • New 500/138-kV substation in Frederick County, VA
  • Construction target: fall 2029, in-service 2030–31
  • Estimated cost: ~$960 million

Zayo Columbus–Ashburn Dark Fiber

  • Announced Nov 2018, built ~2019–2021
  • ~400 route miles, lowest-latency Columbus–Ashburn path
  • Follows North Bend Rail Trail: Parkersburg → Clarksburg
  • Continues east via US-33 corridor through Elkins
  • Terminates at Ashburn / Data Center Alley
  • Underground dark fiber construction
Closest approach: ~9.75 miles — The two routes come nearest to each other at the eastern end, where the MARL line terminates near Gore, VA and the Zayo fiber passes through the Strasburg/Wardensville corridor ~10 miles to the south. Both routes converge on the same Northern Virginia data center market from different directions — MARL brings power from Appalachian generation, Zayo carries data from Midwest cloud hubs.
